# Showcase assets

Wera models and the Rolex watch geometry, optimized representations and posters were imported from the author's spatial-elements-brand-examples project. Wera and Rolex names identify the demonstrated objects; the website is not a shop and is not presented as an official manufacturer website. Manufacturer links and the no-purchase notice appear on the detail pages.

The Three.js watch example was a major motivation for Spatial Elements: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/master/examples/webgl_watch.html

The models are website demonstration assets, not assets included in the npm packages. Source hashes and optimization metadata are recorded in src/lib/showcase/assets.json. Existing third-party asset rights remain with their respective owners.
